# Armored Tanks

3D chase-view tank range and yard editor. The fight stays on a flat metre grid, the same way RING LAW does: cover that stops tracks, shells, or sight, and rivers that stop tracks unless you lay a ford or a bridge.

Hulls are built from dimensions (USA, USSR, Germany). Top-down skin plates are not wrapped onto the meshes.

## Play

- **Start** rolls the selected hull onto the selected yard. Duel, or free drive.
- **W / S** throttle, **A / D** steer (A is left from the chase camera), mouse aims, click or Space fires.
- **Level editor** places trees, buildings, rocks, fences, roads, rivers, fords, and bridges. A yard is playable only when both spawns are dry and a hull-wide path connects them.
- Maps and duel wins stay in this browser (`armored-tanks-maps-v1`, `armored-tanks-scores-v1`).

Rules adapted from [SnakeWard/ring-law](https://github.com/SnakeWard/ring-law). This is a new game, not a fork of that repository.
